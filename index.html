DarkForge

DarkForge is a video production and publishing tool for independent short-video creators. It helps a creator produce original, AI-assisted vertical videos and send them to the TikTok account the creator connects, always after the creator reviews and approves each video.

What it does

  • Writes original short stories and scripts, generates voice, captions and visuals, and renders a vertical video.
  • Lets the creator review the script and the final video before anything is sent anywhere.
  • Connects the creator's own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit (user.info.basic).
  • Uploads the approved video to that account with the Content Posting API (video.upload for drafts, video.publish for direct posts), with the caption and privacy chosen by the creator.

What it does not do

  • It never posts without the creator's approval and never posts on accounts the creator has not connected.
  • It does not read, scrape or store other users' content, followers or personal data.
  • It does not sell or share data. See the Privacy Policy.

All videos are labelled as AI-generated content on the platform. Stories, characters and companies shown are fictional.
