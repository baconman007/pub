Privacy Policy / Política de Privacidade

Last updated / Última atualização: 2026-10-08

Who we are

DarkForge is operated by an independent creator (contact below). It is a tool that creators use to produce and publish their own videos.

What data we handle

  • TikTok account connection: when a creator connects an account, we receive an access token, a refresh token, and basic profile information (display name, avatar) through user.info.basic.
  • Videos and captions that the creator produces and approves for upload.
  • We do not collect data about viewers, followers or any other TikTok users, and we do not use cookies or analytics on this website.

How we use it

Only to upload and publish the creator's approved videos to the creator's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API, and to show which account is connected. Nothing else.

Storage, sharing and retention

  • Tokens and videos are stored on the creator's own computer, not on a shared server.
  • We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, except TikTok itself when the creator asks for an upload.
  • Data is kept while the account is connected. Disconnecting an account deletes its stored tokens.

Your choices

  • Revoke access any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Ask us to delete any data related to you by e-mailing the contact below.

TikTok

Use of TikTok data is also governed by TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
