How it works / Como funciona

1. Connect your TikTok account

The creator clicks Connect and is sent to TikTok's own authorization screen (Login Kit). The app only asks for:

  • user.info.basic — to show which account is connected (name and avatar).
  • video.upload — to send a finished video to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  • video.publish — to post a finished video directly, only after the creator approves it.

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

2. Create and review

The creator picks a story idea; the app writes the script, which the creator can edit or reject. Then it generates voice, captions and visuals and renders the video. The creator watches the final video and approves or rejects it.

3. Publish

Only approved videos can be sent. The creator chooses the caption, who can view the video (privacy level) and whether the video is a draft in the TikTok inbox or a direct post. Videos are marked as AI-generated content. Until the app completes TikTok's audit, posts are limited to private visibility by TikTok itself.

4. Data handling

Access tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer and used only to call the TikTok APIs described above. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Resumo em português
